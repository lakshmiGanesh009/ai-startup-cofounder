2.1The brief, and how it was met

The brief: a founding team of agents that collaborates, not a static plan

The capstone brief asks for an autonomous multi-agent system that simulates a founding team: specialised agents working under a Chief of Staff, with structured reasoning loops, critique and refinement, ending in one launch strategy. The brief is reproduced word for word in 02 Brief, architecture and user flow; this is its shape.

The problem

Startup decisions span product, market, technology, finance and risk; they need debate, iteration and leadership-driven synthesis. Single AI outputs come out isolated and fragmented.

The goal

  • Startup roles as specialised agents: Marketing, Finance, Technical, Risk
  • Structured reasoning loops: ReAct or Plan–Act–Check
  • Hierarchical delegation through a Chief of Staff
  • Critique and refinement cycles
  • One unified launch strategy

Functional requirements

  • Role-based agents: the four specialists
  • Manager-led delegation: a Chief of Staff that coordinates, delegates and synthesises
  • Iterative reasoning: Plan–Act–Check loops, reflection and critique
  • Multi-agent coordination: back-and-forth between agents, conflict resolution and refinement
  • Optimisation: fewer redundant reasoning calls, lower latency, structured outputs

Sample prompts it must handle

"Evaluate this startup idea in the AI healthcare space." "Identify risks in our go-to-market plan." "Refine the pricing strategy after financial review."

Technical details

Python and LangGraph, with langchain, openai / anthropic, pydantic, fastapi (optional), uvicorn and dotenv; the variables OPENAI_API_KEY, ANTHROPIC_API_KEY, MODEL_NAME and DEBUG_MODE.

Deliverables

  • A functional multi-agent system
  • Architecture and workflow design
  • Optimisation and refactoring
  • A demonstration, with an iterative refinement
  • Documentation

Judged on

Coordination, delegation and synthesis, self-correction, workflow clarity and state modelling, optimisation, code quality, documentation.
